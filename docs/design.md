# Design direction

Reading this as a premium consumer rental website for travellers and customers in Ghana. ANLIMA navy, gold and white anchor the identity. Spacious Geist typography, coastal photography and restrained motion establish the public experience. Taste Skill is applied to marketing surfaces; staff workflows use direct forms, accessible controls and practical tables.

Taste dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 6, MOTION_INTENSITY 4, VISUAL_DENSITY 3. Native CSS is used for the owned brand aesthetic. System dark mode and reduced motion are supported. Mobile navigation uses native disclosure and all major grids collapse explicitly. The generated coastal SUV hero is saved in `public/images/anlima-hero.png` and rendered through Next Image. Catalogue photographs and seeded vehicle records are illustrative until ANLIMA supplies approved assets.

Hero asset prompt: photorealistic premium SUV on the right side of a palm-lined Ghana coastal road, warm late-afternoon light, dark empty space on the left for headings, navy shadows and gold sunlight, no text or logos. Generated with the built-in image tool.
